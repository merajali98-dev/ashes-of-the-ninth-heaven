# Design and release notes

## The campaign

Each of the seven regions is written around a person who can testify about a particular abuse of the heavenly ledger. The main route moves from a blank levy notice to the first account. Side expeditions give that conflict local consequences, open techniques, and supply materials. The three teacher choices in Ashen Prefecture establish an early build direction. The final choice defines the ending recorded in the Chronicle.

## The battle loop

A player sees the foe's next move and its projected impact on vitality. Qi limits which techniques can be played this turn. Guard covers the immediate attack, while Ward, Focus, and the other statuses change later decisions. Neutral techniques hold the Resonance chain in place. Three techniques from one school yield a shared draw and Qi bonus plus a school-specific effect.

Combat state uses a seeded shuffle and serializable piles, statuses, and intent position. Guard and triggered effects resolve before the next draw. An opponent with a transition seal reaches each phase boundary and stays protected until that phase's first move. Timed damage respects the seal and preserves the previously announced intent.

## The economy

Expeditions award jade, essence, techniques, and items. The Shop sells all cultivation-eligible techniques and 45 graded card materials; the Workshop forges cards, tempers five shared quality levels, and salvages spare copies. Mission rewards stay usable. Repeatable material commissions supply the long-term grind, with exact school-targeted payouts. Nine layers form each of ten cultivation realms; lifetime essence advances layers and jade funds breakthroughs. Saved decks and editing drafts protect their reserved copies from salvage. Equipment, alchemy, manuals, sect discounts on ordinary goods, and the original expedition rewards remain integrated. See `economy-design.md` and `cultivation-design.md` for formulas and gates.

## Verification

Content tests check registry identities, story links, effect schema, crafting inputs, item availability, and art paths. Combat tests cover effects and timing, including all techniques, opponent intents, consumables, phases, and equipment bonuses. Progression tests cover the journal, economy, limits, save compatibility, and failed actions.

The scripted playtest enters and resolves all 49 expeditions via progression actions. It uses three teacher choices, chooses cards and equipment from earned or purchased stock, and exercises all 35 fights on both difficulty settings. Browser checks exercise onboarding, combat, mid-battle reload, choices, refining, shopping, equipping, recovery, and navigation.

## Art brief

The art was generated with the built-in ImageGen tool and shipped as local WebP files. The shared prompt brief called for original xianxia subjects rendered like contemporary Chinese 3D donghua, with sculpted faces, engraved metal and layered silk, physically based lighting, volumetric Qi, and ancient Chinese architecture set against cosmic machinery. [Battle Through the Heavens animation](https://weibo.com/dpcq2017?lpage=homeRecom) · [Swallowed Star on WeTV](https://wetv.vip/zh-tw/play/0qgqd4kcd9ajxrs)

- **Sanctuary hero.** Shen Luo stands against a broken celestial astrolabe in charcoal titanium and carved gold armor, with a red scarf and a jade sword. Keep the left of the panorama dark for the title.
- **Eclipse arena.** Render a wet obsidian court under a solar eclipse, flanked by suspended Qi rings, a gilded imperial gate, and warm lantern light. Keep the lower field open for the combat board.
- **Regions and sects.** Render each location as a separate atmospheric scene. Preserve the drowned roofs of the Salt Estuary, red curtains of the Hollow Theatre, submerged jade of the Lotus Vault, electrical bridges of Thunder Span, and celestial mechanisms of the Last Observatory and Ninth Gate.
- **Items.** Render every one of the 231 items as an individual prop with a readable silhouette and distinct materials. The 32 original signature items include all 18 legendary objects. The icon pass adds 199 square illustrations for the remaining items.
- **Cast and techniques.** Use consistent facial structure, garments, and equipment across character appearances. Give each of the 597 techniques an individual image based on its name, school, and effect, with a strong central action readable at card size. Each of the twelve card types has a sculpted, contrasting central emblem on the shared rounded jade frame.

## Card construction

Technique cards share the softened scroll-tablet outline established by Gather Qi and Empty Jar. Rounded shoulders, a split crown, broad bases, and thick polished jade rims unite the set. Type crests and artwork mounts distinguish Strikes, Wards, Mantras, and Remedies.

Common, uncommon, rare, and legendary cards carry one, two, three, and five rarity seals. Pale jade, emerald jade, blue jade, and warm golden jade distinguish the finishes. Layered bevels, mineral veins, cabochon crests, and carved relief add depth. The same frame appears in the hand, collection, inspection panel, and casting animation. Costs sit on the outer frame so the artwork window cannot obscure them.
