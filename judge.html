The merchants' agents

Once a market day each merchant decides what to keep, in character through NEAR AI (or by its rules if the model is unavailable), and signs it from its own account with a key that can only call merchant_set_strategy. The contract clamps every target to 60–160%.

  • Loading…

Merchants hiring

A merchant short of a good posts one paid job from its own account (merchant_post_job, the same limited agent key). The contract checks the terms against the rules file (pay between its bid and its own valuation, at most 10% of its purse) and holds the wages in escrow until a player delivers or a market day passes.

  • Loading…

Haggles

The contract decides every haggle with haggle() from the rules file: accept, one counter, or walk away. NEAR AI only voices the answer, and its line is used only if it states the decided price exactly.

Fast-forward a week

The same rules and engine, played by simulated traders for 7 days in a minute. Nothing is sent to the chain; it is labelled as a simulation.

Where each requirement lives

NEP-245 itemsOne contract holds jade and all 873 goods: contracts/items. Every balance change emits a standard nep245 event.
Social or email loginGoogle, an emailed code, or guest; the server makes a testnet account and the browser's own key can only call the market (server/api.js, js/online/signer.js).
One pricing filejs/market/merchant-rules.js: the tooltip, the fast-forward and (generated into Rust) the contract all use it; tests/parity.rs replays 3,218 steps exactly.
Fast-forward/#market-sim: a week of trading in a minute, with flood and audit scenarios.
NFTs that are shopsShop Deeds (NEP-171): the holder takes 10% of each profitable day into the deed's vault and runs the shop or appoints its agent; the vault moves with the deed. docs/shop-deeds.md
AI agents that tradeEach merchant is an account with an agent key limited to two methods: it sets its strategy daily and hires players for its shortages, in character through NEAR AI, within limits the contract enforces. Players haggle; the rules decide and the agent speaks. docs/agents.md
Game loop on paperdocs/game-loop.md: the 30-second, 5-minute and session loops.
