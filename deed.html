Shop Deeds

Each shop in the Night Bazaar has one deed. Whoever holds it takes the owner's share of each profitable market day, and runs the shop or lets its merchant's agent run it. The Honest Ledger keeps every deed's history: who held it, what the shop paid them, and what they claimed. Back to the game.
