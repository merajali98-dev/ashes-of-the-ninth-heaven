Privacy

Ashes of the Ninth Heaven is a hackathon game running on NEAR testnet. Last updated 7 November 2026.

What we keep

  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account ID, your email address and your first name, taken from the sign-in token Google gives the game. We ask for nothing else.
  • If you sign in by email: your email address, and a one-time code that expires after 15 minutes.
  • As a guest: nothing personal. Your guest session is a cookie on your device.
  • Your game account: a NEAR testnet account (for example shen-luo.ashes-ninth.testnet) and the public half of your browser's key. The server keeps an encrypted recovery key so you can sign in again on another device.

What is public

Trades, rewards and patronage are recorded on NEAR testnet, a public ledger, under your game account. Your email and name are never written there; account names are either the name you choose or a random ID.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking cookies. The only cookie is the sign-in session.

Deleting your data

Ask us through the project repository and we will delete your sign-in record and session. Entries already on the public testnet ledger cannot be removed.
